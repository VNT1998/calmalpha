## 2. Services
Section title: **What we do**
Intro: End-to-end software and AI engineering, from first idea to production.

1. **Custom software development**
   Web applications, APIs, internal tools and full-stack products, from architecture through deployment.
2. **AI agents and automation**
   Multi-step agents that plan, use tools and hand off to people when it matters, built with explicit state so behaviour is traceable and testable.
3. **Voice and conversational AI**
   Phone and chat agents for outreach, support and scheduling, with defined call flows, fallbacks and human escalation.
4. **Knowledge and retrieval systems**
   Retrieval-augmented assistants grounded in your own content, such as websites and documents, with answers you can trace back to a source.
5. **AI strategy and architecture**
   Model selection, cost optimization, evaluation design and build-versus-buy advice, so AI spending turns into outcomes.
6. **Delivery and cloud**
   Containerized deployments, CI/CD pipelines and production hardening, so what we build keeps running.

## 3. Process
Section title: **How we work with you**
1. **Discover.** We learn the problem, the users and the constraints, and agree what success looks like.
2. **Design.** We propose the architecture, scope and timeline, and prototype the riskiest parts first.
3. **Build.** We deliver in short iterations you can see and test, with code you own.
4. **Ship and support.** We deploy, monitor and improve, and hand over documentation your team can use.

## 4. Technology
Section title: **How we build**

## 5. Principles
Section title: **Principles**
1. **Calm over hype.** We say what a system can and cannot do, and we measure it.
2. **Reliable before clever.** Simple, testable logic wherever the problem is well-defined; models where judgement is needed.
3. **Cost is a feature.** Every pipeline is designed with a cost per task in mind.
4. **People stay in the loop.** Agents escalate to humans for decisions that matter.

## /responsible-ai
- **Title:** Responsible AI | Calm Alpha
- **Description:** How Calm Alpha approaches safe, accountable AI development.

**Our approach.** We build AI systems that people can understand, check and control. These are the commitments we hold ourselves to.

1. **Human oversight.** Systems that act on behalf of people include clear escalation to a human for consequential decisions.
2. **Transparency.** Users are told when they are interacting with an AI system, including on voice and chat agents.
3. **Accuracy and grounding.** Where possible, answers are grounded in source material the user can inspect, and we test for errors before release.
4. **Privacy.** We collect the minimum data a system needs, and we do not train models on client data without explicit agreement.
5. **Acceptable use.** We do not build systems for deception, harassment, unlawful surveillance or other harmful uses, and we follow the usage policies of the model providers we rely on.
6. **Accountability.** If something goes wrong, we investigate, fix it and tell affected parties.

**Report a concern.** Email vinit.k@calmalpha.in.
